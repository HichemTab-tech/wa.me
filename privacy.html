Tap to wa.me

Privacy Policy

This app is built to quickly open a WhatsApp chat from a phone number. It reads clipboard text locally to make that flow faster, and it does not keep or transmit your personal data.

Clipboard Access

Tap to wa.me may read text from your device clipboard when the app opens or when you press the paste action. This is used only to prefill the phone number field.

What We Do Not Do

  • We do not store clipboard content.
  • We do not send clipboard content to any server.
  • We do not share clipboard content with third parties.
  • We do not create accounts or collect analytics in this privacy flow.

How the App Works

After reading and cleaning the number locally on your device, the app opens a standard wa.me link so your chosen messaging app or browser can continue the conversation flow.
